FAQ

The future of business automation, explained

Simple answers to how Yapli AI operates for your business.

Can I use my existing WhatsApp Business number?

Yes. You can connect an existing number via Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, or register a dedicated business SIM. Meta's official API ensures your number is safe from bans.

How do I update prices, services or salon hours?

Simply edit your Google Sheet! In the Business Info tab, type your new price or timing. The AI Receptionist reads it in real-time on the very next customer message. Zero coding needed.

Does it understand voice notes and regional languages?

Yes! Powered by Gemini 3.8 Flash, Yapli AI downloads customer audio voice notes, transcribes them automatically, and responds naturally in Tamil, Hindi, Tanglish/Hinglish, or English.

What happens if a customer wants to talk to a human?

If a customer has a complaint or asks for a manager, the bot gracefully hands off, notifies you immediately via Telegram or WhatsApp, and pauses autopilot so you can reply manually.
